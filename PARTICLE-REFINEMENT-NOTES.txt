AXON particle visual refinement

Modified files:
- src/webgl/shaders.ts
- src/webgl/AxonParticleScene.ts

Changes:
- Reduced GPU and CPU fallback point-size multiplier from 0.045 to 0.018.
- Added per-particle size variation.
- Reduced particle base brightness and made lavender highlights rarer.
- Added soft radial sprite falloff and controlled alpha for additive blending.
- Made the hollow ring SDF subtly irregular with variable ring/tube radii and softer secondary lobe.

The particle count, GPU simulation, fluid simulation, pointer interaction, UI, and media were left unchanged.

Note: This is a code-level refinement; visual appearance should be checked in the target browser/GPU and may need small tuning adjustments.
